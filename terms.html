Journal Junkie terms of use

Last updated October 8, 2026

Using the app

Journal Junkie is a personal journaling and planning app. You're responsible for what you enter and for keeping your sign-in secure. Don't use the app for anything illegal or to store other people's private information without their permission.

Your content

What you write stays yours. We store it only to run the app for you, as described in the privacy policy.

Grade estimates

Grade impact rankings, streaks and other numbers are estimates based on what you enter. They aren't official grades and may not match your school's calculations.

Availability

We work to keep Journal Junkie running, but it's provided as is, without guarantees. Features may change, and the service may occasionally be unavailable.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Me tab. We may suspend accounts that misuse the service.
