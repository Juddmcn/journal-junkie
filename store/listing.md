## Description
Journal Junkie is a nightly check-in for students who want to stay on top of school, training and life without a complicated planner.

EVERY NIGHT, IN A MINUTE
• Log hours for each part of your life: school, practice, work, whatever matters to you
• Mark your workout done, skipped or a planned rest day
• Check off your daily habits
• Rate your mood and sleep, and write a quick journal entry
• Look back through past entries any time

YOUR TO-DOS, SORTED
• Paste your Canvas calendar link and new assignments show up automatically
• Homework and quizzes & tests are split into their own lists
• Add your own to-dos with due dates, priorities and repeats
• Count down to big dates, like midterms or a meeting, and see how much of your list is done

KNOW WHAT MATTERS MOST
• Grade impact ranks every assignment by how much it's worth toward your grade
• Enter each class's weights once, and log scores as they come in
• Track which assignments you used AI support on

WATCH YOURSELF IMPROVE
• Two streaks: days logged, and days you hit every goal
• Charts for to-do completion, hours by area, sleep, mood and lift PRs
• A nightly reminder so you never forget to check in

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your journal is tied to your account and isn't visible to other users. Delete your account and everything in it anytime from the Me tab.
