Journal Junkie support

Need a hand? Here are answers to the common questions.

How do I add Journal Junkie to my home screen?

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen." If you downloaded it from the App Store, it's already there.

How do I connect Canvas?

In Canvas on a computer, open Calendar, then click "Calendar Feed" at the bottom right and copy the link. In Journal Junkie, go to Me, then Calendar links, tap "Add calendar link," and paste it. New assignments show up on your To-do tab automatically.

How do I connect Google Calendar?

On a computer, open Google Calendar settings, pick your calendar, and copy "Secret address in iCal format." Paste it in Me, then Calendar links, and choose "Events."

What counts toward my streak?

A day counts when you hit every goal you set: your hours goals for each area, your workout (a planned rest day counts, up to the limit you choose), each habit, and every to-do due that day.

What does "grade impact" mean?

It estimates how much each assignment is worth toward its class grade, using the weights you enter for that class on the Me tab. Classes where your average is lower get a boost, since there's more to gain there.

How do I delete my data or my account?

On the Me tab, tap "Erase all my data" to clear what you've entered, or "Delete account" to permanently delete your account and everything in it.
